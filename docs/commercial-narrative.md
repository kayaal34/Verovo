# Agent spend audit and value measurement

Updated 2026-11-23. [Product definition](product-definition.md).

Verovo serves teams accountable for AI agent spending and results. Business and engineering owners explain the work; finance and procurement help with spend and approval; control tests also require an authorized execution owner. Identify who provides records, uses the conclusion and can commission work.

## Three tasks with independent purchase reasons

| Task | Result | Commercial hypothesis |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Period charges and delivery | Recalculate supported charges, review delivery and produce line-level spend actions with owner roles | Bounded project fee; success-based terms only for a suitable correction case |
| Control effectiveness | Compare stop receipts with subsequent charges, late settlement and bypass paths | Scoped validation or recurring review for an authorized workflow; no hard-budget guarantee |
| Private procurement comparison | Fix the agent and acceptance rubric, compare two tools with full task cost and uncertainty | Bounded comparison project, reused only if it supports an actual procurement decision |

These tasks can be commissioned separately by different customers and owners. None is a launched fixed-price package. Scope, inputs, deliverables and any fee are agreed before commissioned work. A fit discussion is not an order. Existing sample-based [measurement runs](https://roy-tong.github.io/Verovo/services.html) remain free; old $10/$20 diagnostics are not current offers.

## Value and cost

Separate findings, vendor recognition, actual refunds, used credits, control receipts, subsequent charges, review hours, buyer decisions and collected service fees. Do not count a proposed claim, an issued-but-unused credit or an intercepted estimate as realized recovery or savings. Do not add the same benefit twice. A useful review can confirm no difference or remain INCONCLUSIVE.

Include acquisition, unsuccessful screening, initial mapping, review, follow-up and maintenance in delivery cost. If every engagement requires substantial new work, treat it as a service rather than claiming software scalability. A future renewal opportunity not yet due is neither churn nor proof of retention. A comparison reused in procurement is stronger evidence than a generated benchmark report, but it is not yet an industry measurement network.

## Provider and technical work

Providers auditing upstream spend are buyers in that transaction. Downstream provider work needs a concrete customer dispute, revenue discrepancy or procurement request. State the commissioning party and retain findings in both directions. Shared technical infrastructure is not proof of two-sided demand.

Open-source rules, adapters, conformance and diagnostics support reproducibility, technical collaboration and distribution. Adoption, fixes and test results have value without being counted as enterprise purchases. OTel/FOCUS mappings, LiteLLM/Langfuse imports, offline mitmproxy and Inspect runners follow actual task blockers rather than forming a mandatory platform. A public ranking, payment network or cross-customer measurement panel remains a separate future hypothesis.

## Current delivery

The local buyer review combines user-defined criteria, observations, separate conclusions and offline reports. `spend-actions` turns an existing Audit Case into a line-level worklist without executing or authorizing payment. `provider check` validates adapter manifests without claiming live connectivity. Task economics connects direct and allocated cost to verified outcomes. Human review establishes applicable rules, business criteria and actual disposition.
